# 🍔 GrillZilla 💥

Welcome to **GrillZilla**, a tiny Python food-ordering system where you can build your order, watch your bill grow, and finally checkout. 💸

## 🍔 What's Cooking?

Choose what you want from the menu:

* 🍔 Burger — $7
* 🍟 Fries — $3
* 🥤 Coke — $1

Pick an item, enter the quantity, and GrillZilla keeps adding everything to your bill.

Don't want anything else? Just hit **4** and checkout! 🧾
